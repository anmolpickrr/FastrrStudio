    How it works

    If you skipped the walk.

    Four steps from a product page to a library of finished assets. You only do the first one.

    1. Paste a link

      A product link, or a few photos. The agent imports your photos, your logo and your brand facts.

    2. Script, checked

      A timed script with every line matched to a real photo. A line no photo supports is rewritten.

    3. Rendered on the right engine

      Each shot goes to the engine that suits it. Ask for another look at any time.

    4. Delivered, ready to change

      Everything lands in one library. Swap one clip, extend to 40 seconds, or just say what you want.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    What do I need to get started?

    One product link, or a few product photos. The agent reads the page for your photos, logo and brand facts. There is nothing to fill in and nothing to upload twice.

    What can the agent make?

    Reels, 360° spins, catalogue shots, statics in three sizes, five-card carousels, landing pages and campaign kits in Indian languages. Songs and music videos too.

    How does it stay on brand?

    Your logo is placed exactly, never redrawn. Colours are read from your site. Every line of the script is checked against your photos, and any line your photos don't support is rewritten before anything renders.

    Can I change things after they're made?

    Yes. Swap one shot and keep the rest, stretch a 20-second reel to 40 seconds from the last frame, or ask for another engine. You can type or just say what you want.

    Which languages do you cover?

    Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi and more. Each cut gets its own voice, captions and statics from the same footage, with the logo and timing kept.

    Which AI engines do you use?

    Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Wan 3.0, Omni 1.1 Flash, HeyGen and Higgsfield Cinema Studio. The agent picks the one that suits each shot.

    Does it work for agencies?

    Yes. Your team shares one library and the same conversations, and you can bulk-download everything made for a brand in a given week.
