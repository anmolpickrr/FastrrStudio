    How it works

    The short version.

    Four steps from product page to finished campaign. You only do the first one.

    1. Share a link

      Send a product page or a few photos. We pull in your images, logo and product details.

    2. Get a script

      A timed script where every line is backed by a real photo of your product.

    3. See it made

      Each shot is made on the engine that suits it best. Ask for a different look anytime.

    4. Make it yours

      Everything lands in one library. Change a shot, stretch a reel or add a language.

    Questions

    Questions, answered.

    What do I need to get started?

    Just a product link or a few photos. We read the page for your images, logo and product details, so there’s no form to fill in.

    What can you make?

    Reels, statics, AI GIFs, 360° spins, catalogue shots, carousels, landing pages, brand kits and campaign kits in Indian languages. Songs and music videos too.

    How do you keep it on brand?

    We place your logo exactly as it is and take your colours from your site. Every line of the script is checked against your photos before anything is made.

    Can I ask for changes?

    Of course. Swap one shot and keep the rest, stretch a 20-second reel to 40 seconds, or try a different look. Type it, or just say it.

    Which languages do you work in?

    Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, English and more. Each version gets its own voiceover, captions and statics, with your logo and timing kept.

    Which tools do you use?

    Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Wan 3.0, Omni 1.1 Flash, HeyGen and Higgsfield Cinema Studio. We pick the one that suits each shot, so you don’t have to.

    Do you work with agencies?

    Yes. Your team shares one library and every conversation, and you can download a brand’s whole week of work in one go.
